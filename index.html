Open on two devices with microphones. Host creates a session and shares the Peer ID. Join connects.
Place devices a known distance apart (set baseline). Make sounds; the radar estimates source position via time-difference of arrival (TDOA).
Use calibration ping for better clock sync. Accuracy depends on quiet room, similar mic gains, and low network latency.
Note: Browser mics have processing (AGC, noise suppression) that can affect timing. Disable if possible for better results.
